# Design Context for Blackjack Game

## Users
Casual players (friends) playing together for fun. They want a quick, enjoyable experience without friction or confusion. Usage context: varied devices (phones, tablets, desktops) at different times, including in portrait mode on mobile during active play.

## Brand Personality
**Playful, accessible, fun** — the interface should feel inviting and easy to use, not intimidating. Comfortable and social.

## Aesthetic Direction
**Dark felt-table casino aesthetic** with gold/beige accents (#c9a84c). Maintain the existing green felt background and gold typography. Not trying to be hyper-realistic or luxury — it's a friendly digital table.

## Design Principles
1. **Mobile-first portrait mode** — optimize for phone held vertically (primary constraint)
2. **Touch-friendly interactions** — chips and buttons must be large enough to tap confidently without mistakes
3. **Full visibility without scrolling** — keep dealer hand, player hand, and betting controls visible simultaneously in portrait
4. **Responsive scaling** — elements grow/shrink gracefully across phone, tablet, and desktop sizes
5. **Maintain playful feel** — keep the gold accents, animations, and casual tone; don't make it sterile or minimal
